## What is TIGON Wild?
TIGON Wild is a brand by TIGON Golf Carts LLC that specializes in off-road, street-legal, and all-wheel drive (AWD) golf carts built for adventure. They manufacture and sell premium electric golf carts designed to handle extreme terrain, meet street-legal requirements, and provide all-wheel drive capability.

## What makes TIGON Wild different from regular golf carts?
TIGON Wild carts are engineered for adventure, not just golf courses. They feature lifted frames with 6-inch lift kits, heavy-duty suspension, all-terrain tires, LED light bars, sealed waterproof electronics, brush guards, and skid plates. They can handle trails, mud, sand, mountains, and water crossings. Some models are also street-legal with DOT-approved lights, mirrors, seatbelts, and VIN plates.

## What types of golf carts does TIGON Wild offer?
TIGON Wild offers three main product lines:
1. Off-Road Series: Lifted frames, all-terrain tires, heavy-duty suspension, brush guards, skid plates for trails, mud, and mountains.
2. Street-Legal Series: DOT headlights, turn signals, mirrors, seatbelts, windshield, and VIN plate for legal road use up to 35 mph zones.
3. All-Wheel Drive (AWD) Series: Power to all four wheels for maximum grip on sand, mud, wet grass, steep hills, and loose gravel.

# INDUSTRY TERMINOLOGY
# ============================================================

- LSV: Low-Speed Vehicle, a street-legal vehicle with max speed of 25 mph, legal on roads up to 35 mph
- DOT: Department of Transportation, federal safety standard compliance
- AWD: All-Wheel Drive, power delivered to all four wheels simultaneously
- Lift Kit: Suspension modification to increase ground clearance
- Skid Plate: Protective plate on the underside of the vehicle to protect components from rocks and obstacles
- Brush Guard: Front-mounted protective bar to deflect branches and debris
- Lithium-Ion: Advanced battery technology providing longer range and faster charging than lead-acid
- EV: Electric Vehicle
